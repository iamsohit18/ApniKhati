# 🌾 ApniKheti

### **Everything Your Farm Needs. In One Place.**

ApniKheti is a **multilingual AI-powered AgriTech platform** designed to connect farmers with the agricultural information, services, markets, experts, and opportunities they need throughout the farming lifecycle.

From **crop planning and weather advisory to equipment rental, soil testing, storage, buyers, market prices, AI assistance, and government schemes/subsidies**, ApniKheti brings essential agricultural services together on one platform.

---

## 🚜 Vision

> **One Farmer. One AI. One Agricultural Ecosystem.**

Our vision is to make technology and agricultural opportunities more accessible to farmers, regardless of their language, location, or level of digital literacy.

ApniKheti aims to become a digital agriculture ecosystem connecting:

**Farmers ↔ AI ↔ Markets ↔ Buyers ↔ Equipment ↔ Storage ↔ Experts ↔ Government Support**

## 🧠 AI Scheme Matching

Farmers can ask:

Each program can display:

* Required documents
* Application process

# 🔥 Core Concept

ApniKheti is designed around a simple idea:

A farmer can ask:

> *"I have 3 acres of land and grow wheat. What should I do today, where should I sell, what equipment do I need, what storage is available, and what government support can I get?"*

**Farmer Profile + AI + Weather + Soil + Market + Buyers + Equipment + Storage + Policies + Schemes + Subsidies**

into one personalized agricultural experience.

# 🛡️ Data & Information Reliability

Agricultural policies, schemes, subsidies, market prices and weather information can change.

Therefore, ApniKheti is designed to:

* Prioritize official sources
* Display source information
* Show last verified date
* Track policy updates
* Clearly distinguish demo data from live data
* Avoid presenting unverified information as fact

AI-generated recommendations should be clearly distinguished from verified information.
